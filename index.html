The problem

The web was built for eyes and mouse clicks. Agents have neither.

Online stores are full of pop‑ups, dynamic widgets, and flows designed for humans. When an AI agent visits, it gets lost, guesses, or leaves — and the sale goes to whoever is easiest to read.

Invisible

Agent traffic doesn't show up as customers in your analytics — you never see the sales you lost.

Fragile

Agents that scrape and click break every time your theme, layout or checkout changes.

Winner‑takes‑all

Agents favor the stores they can transact with reliably. Early movers get the default slot.
